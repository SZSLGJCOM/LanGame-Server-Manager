# Code of Conduct

[English](#english) | [简体中文](#简体中文)

## English

### Expected behavior

Project participants must:

- discuss code, design, operations, and evidence without attacking people;
- give specific, actionable review feedback;
- respect different experience levels, languages, identities, and backgrounds;
- protect private reports, credentials, player data, and other sensitive information;
- acknowledge mistakes and correct harmful behavior.

### Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, personal insults, doxxing, and publishing private communication without permission are not accepted. Repeated disruption, bad-faith review, or knowingly submitting malicious code or fabricated evidence is also unacceptable.

### Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, issue trackers, project chat, community events, and public communication made on behalf of the project.

### Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through the repository host. If no private contact method is available, use the [private contact request form](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/contact_request.yml) without naming people or describing the incident. The resulting issue is public.

Maintainers will limit access to the report, assess available evidence, avoid conflicts of interest, and take proportionate action. Actions may include a private warning, content removal, participation restrictions, or removal from project spaces. Retaliation against a reporter or witness is prohibited.

## 简体中文

### 参与要求

项目参与者应当：

- 围绕代码、设计、运维和证据讨论，不攻击个人；
- 提供具体、可执行的审查意见；
- 尊重不同经验、语言、身份和背景；
- 保护私密报告、凭据、玩家数据和其他敏感信息；
- 承认错误，并纠正造成伤害的行为。

### 禁止行为

项目不接受骚扰、威胁、歧视、性化关注、蓄意恐吓、人身侮辱、公开他人隐私，或未经允许发布私下沟通内容。反复扰乱协作、恶意审查，以及明知故犯地提交恶意代码或伪造证据，同样属于禁止行为。

### 适用范围

本规范适用于仓库讨论、代码审查、Issue、项目群聊、社区活动，以及代表本项目进行的公开沟通。

### 报告与处理

请通过代码托管平台私下联系维护者并报告行为问题。如果没有私下联系渠道，请使用[私下联系请求表单](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/contact_request.yml)，不得写出相关人员姓名或事件细节。生成的 Issue 为公开内容。

维护者会限制报告访问范围，核对现有证据，回避利益冲突，并采取与问题程度相称的措施，包括私下提醒、移除内容、限制参与或移出项目空间。禁止报复报告人或证人。

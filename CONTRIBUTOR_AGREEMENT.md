## English

### 1. Parties and scope

This agreement is between the contributor who expressly accepts it and LanGame
Team, acting through the maintainers authorized to represent the official
LanGame Server Manager project ("Official Maintainers"). It applies only to
the original code, documentation, translations, tests, or other material
identified in that contributor's acceptance record ("Contribution").

"Project" means LanGame Server Manager and its official versions. It does not
include unrelated products or independent downstream projects. "You" means the
rightsholder or a representative actually authorized to make these grants on
that rightsholder's behalf.

### 2. You retain ownership

You retain copyright in your Contribution and may independently use or license
your own original material. This agreement does not transfer copyright or give
you rights in the rest of the Project. Preserve other authors' notices and
separately licensed material.

### 3. Permission for the official Project

For the duration of copyright, you grant the Official Maintainers a worldwide,
nonexclusive, royalty-free copyright license to use, copy, build, review,
modify, incorporate, display, and distribute your Contribution and their
modifications of it as part of the Project, including official modified
versions. This includes commercial use, paid official versions, and commercial
deployment, hosting, maintenance, and support of the Project.

Official versions may continue to be offered to the public under the
[LanGame Source-Available License](LICENSE). The Official Maintainers may also
provide customers with Project versions containing your Contribution and grant
them separate written permission for commercial use of those versions. This
limited sublicensing permission may extend the commercial-use rights in
section 3 of LICENSE; it does not authorize customers to sublicense the
Project or independently distribute modified versions.

The grant may not be withdrawn for uses within this agreed scope. No royalty,
revenue share, employment, support obligation, or promise to accept a
Contribution arises from this agreement. A separate written agreement may
provide compensation.

### 4. Limits on the grant

This agreement does not authorize using your Contribution in unrelated
products, selling it separately from the Project, or replacing the public
source license with arbitrary other terms. Rights beyond section 3 require
separate permission from the relevant rightsholders.

The ordinary public does not receive commercial rights merely because a
Contribution was accepted. The Project's restrictions on independent modified
releases and its [brand use rules](NOTICE) remain applicable; this agreement
does not expand customers' brand permissions.

Third-party material remains under its own license. You grant only rights
you actually hold or are authorized to grant, and no third-party rights are
overridden. No express patent or trademark license is granted by this
agreement beyond rights necessarily implied by applicable law.

### 5. Contribution source and authority

Identify copied or separately licensed material and disclose known rights or
restrictions relevant to the proposed use. Do not claim that third-party code,
game assets, or generated output is yours merely because you submit it.

If an employer, client, or coauthor holds relevant rights, obtain their
authorization or identify the separate permissions needed before acceptance.
A PR author cannot grant another contributor's rights without that authority.
Remove secrets, player data, and materials you are not entitled to submit.

### 6. Explicit acceptance and records

Read this version before submitting a Contribution for inclusion. Actively
confirm agreement in your own PR description or comment using the confirmation
in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), identifying version 1.0 and the covered
contribution or commits. Do not have a maintainer tick the box for you.

Before merge, the Official Maintainers must retain a reviewable record of the
confirmation account or authorized representative, confirmation time, agreement
version and exact text or immutable reference, covered commit IDs, and PR or
patch reference. New material or changed rights after confirmation requires
renewed confirmation for the affected scope.

This record is a review requirement, not an automatic finding that identity,
ownership, or legal authority has been proven. Check unresolved ownership
before merging; passing CI is not a substitute for contributor acceptance.

### 7. Earlier contributions and other communications

Opening a PR, reporting a bug, discussing an idea, making private modifications,
or remaining silent does not by itself accept this agreement. It does not
automatically cover all future submissions.

Earlier contributions retain their existing grants, including any valid MIT
rights. This agreement covers an earlier contribution only if the relevant
rightsholder expressly accepts it for that identified contribution. Updating
this file does not alter an earlier acceptance or authorize new uses by itself.

### 8. Disclaimer

Contributions are provided "AS IS", without warranties or any duty to provide
maintenance or support, to the extent permitted by applicable law. This does
not exclude rights or liabilities that applicable law does not allow to be
excluded. Nothing here promises acceptance, payment, or official endorsement.

[品牌使用规则](NOTICE) 继续适用；本协议不扩大客户的品牌使用权限。

说明复制或单独许可的材料，披露已知且与预期用途有关的权利或限制。不能因为提交了

无权提供的材料。

提交贡献供接纳前，先阅读本版本协议。由你本人在 PR 正文或评论中主动使用
[贡献指南](CONTRIBUTING.md)中的确认声明，明确协议版本 1.0 及所覆盖的贡献或提交。
维护者不能代你勾选。

合并前，官方维护者须保留可核查的记录：确认账号或授权代表、确认时间、协议版本与
准确正文或不可变引用、所覆盖的提交 ID，以及 PR 或补丁引用。确认后新增材料或权利
来源发生变化时，应对受影响范围补充确认。

这是一项审查要求，不代表系统已自动证明身份、权属或授权资格。合并前须处理未解决的
权属问题；CI 通过不能代替贡献者同意。

旧贡献继续保留原有授权，包括已经有效授予的 MIT 权利。只有相关权利人明确接受并
